UN Sustainable Development Goals

How FoodConnect advances the SDGs

We’re aligned with multiple SDGs. Here’s how our workflows map to them.

SDG 2

Zero Hunger

  • Connects surplus meals with orphanages in real time.
  • Tracks quantity & timing to meet immediate needs.
  • Verification reduces missed deliveries and waste.
SDG 12

Responsible Consumption & Production

  • Reduces edible food waste from restaurants and schools.
  • Planned pickups aligned to food safety windows.
  • Encourages reporting and continuous improvement.
SDG 17

Partnerships for the Goals

  • Unites donors, orphanages, volunteers into one workflow.
  • Enables local communities to collaborate and verify.
  • Open to NGOs & civic partners.
Getting started

Three simple steps

  1. Join as a donor, orphanage, or volunteer.
  2. Post your offer/need with time & location.
  3. Connect via verified requests and complete handover.
